I work on **radio frequency fingerprint identification (RFFI)** and **open-set recognition for UAVs** — teaching models to identify *whose* signal it is, and to say *"I don't know"* when it isn't.

### Research

- **RFFI** — extracting stable device fingerprints from raw IQ under channel and receiver variation
- **Cross-domain generalization** — receiver-agnostic models, semantic augmentation, domain shift
- **Open-set recognition** — rejecting unknown emitters instead of forcing them into known classes
- **Multimodal perception** — image, acoustic and IQ signals for UAV sensing
